## 1. 摄影类风格

### 电影剧照 (Cinematic Film Still)
- **关键词**：cinematic film still, dramatic lighting, shallow depth of field, anamorphic lens, film grain, cinematic color grading, teal and orange
- **适用**：故事叙述、角色肖像、产品宣传、任何需要"大片感"的画面
- **提示词标签**：cinematic film still of [主体], dramatic lighting, shallow depth of field, anamorphic lens, film grain, cinematic color grading

### 纪实街拍摄影 (Documentary Street Photography)
- **关键词**：documentary street photography, candid moment, natural light, 35mm focal length, urban backdrop layers, motion blur suggestion, gritty contrast
- **适用**：人文纪实、街头故事、城市生活记录
- **提示词标签**：documentary street photography, candid moment, natural light, [场景], 35mm focal length

### 时尚杂志摄影 (Editorial Fashion Photography)
- **关键词**：editorial fashion photography, Vogue style lighting, studio backdrop, high-key photography, clean background
- **适用**：时尚大片、品牌画册、杂志封面
- **提示词标签**：editorial fashion photography, [主体], Vogue style lighting, studio backdrop

### 自然微距摄影 (Macro Nature Photography)
- **关键词**：extreme macro photography, ultra detailed, shallow focus, macro shot, bokeh
- **适用**：昆虫、植物、水滴、纹理细节拍摄
- **提示词标签**：extreme macro photography of [主体], ultra detailed, shallow focus

### 复古国家地理照片 (Vintage National Geographic)
- **关键词**：1970s National Geographic photography, documentary style, natural lighting, wildlife telephoto capture, 200mm+ lens compression
- **适用**：自然主题、野生动物、探索类题材
